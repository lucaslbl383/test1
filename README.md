# A Level Electric Field Explorer

An interactive web tool that helps A Level students develop an intuitive understanding of electric fields through exploration.

## How to open

Double-click `index.html`, or right-click the file in VS Code and open it in a browser.

## Features

- Switch between a positive charge, negative charge, electric dipole, two like charges and a charged plate
- Drag the field probe to measure electric field strength, direction and potential
- Show or hide equipotential lines over the electric field diagram
- Adjust the test charge and observe the magnitude and direction of the electric force
- Use Free exploration to add, move and remove up to 12 positive or negative charges while the field lines and equipotential lines update live
- Highlight electric field lines in yellow, zoom the diagram, and adjust equipotential-line spacing
